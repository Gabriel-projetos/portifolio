## Guidelines and Scope of Work

To guarantee the standard of technical and aesthetic excellence I deliver in every project, I conduct my services based on the following fundamental principles:

- **Professional Portfolio Showcase**: I exclusively accept projects that can be featured in my public portfolio upon completion, serving as showcases for success cases (except in specific situations previously negotiated under formal confidentiality/NDA terms).

- **Modern Technologies**: I do not perform development, maintenance, or customization on legacy WordPress themes. I prioritize the use of modern technology stacks that are extremely fast, robust, and shielded against security vulnerabilities.

- **Visual Alignment**: The project methodology is built upon deep reference analysis and continuous alignment with the client, ensuring the final design precisely expresses the essence of the brand.

## Infrastructure Management and CMS (Optional): Starting at R$50/month

Many infrastructure platforms and cloud services bill their subscriptions in foreign currency (USD) and have complex billing rules. To save you from financial or technical bureaucracy, I take over the management of all infrastructure required to keep your website online, secure, and up to date. This service includes:

- **Domain (Website address)**: Support during the lookup process, availability check, and purchase mediation with official registrars (such as Cloudflare or Registro.br), renewed annually.

- **Hosting and Server**: Depending on the chosen technology ecosystem, monthly server allocation may be required. By utilizing modern static site architectures or headless CMS, we can optimize the infrastructure so operational costs remain minimal or fully zeroed out.

- **CMS (Content Panel)**: Implementation of modern, intuitive admin panels, giving you and your team total autonomy to update text, images, and new website content at any time—eliminating any reliance on technical support for day-to-day tasks.
